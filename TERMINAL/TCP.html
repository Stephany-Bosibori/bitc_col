Transmission Control Protocol / Internet Protocol (TCP/IP)

The Foundation of Connectivity

TCP/IP is the "language" of the internet. It is a suite of communication protocols used to interconnect network devices on the internet. It defines how data is packetized, addressed, transmitted, routed, and received.

The Role of TCP.

TCP is responsible for data integrity. It breaks large files into smaller "packets," numbers them, and sends them. On the receiving end, it checks for missing packets and requests retransmission if necessary.

The Role of IP.

IP is the addressing protocol. It ensures that packets are routed to the correct destination by assigning unique IP addresses to every device on a network.
