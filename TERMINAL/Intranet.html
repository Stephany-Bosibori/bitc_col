Interanet: Internal Focus

An intranet is a private network accessible only to an organization's members, employees, or others with authorization. It is used to share information and resources within the organization. An Intranet is a private network used within a single organization. It is used for internal communication, sharing policies, and employee collaboration and is protected by firewalls.
